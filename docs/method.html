Method

Chain

1. Stock

Floors are ceil(height / storey height) with storey height set per use class, clipped to [1, 90]. Floor area is footprint × floors. Archetype comes from the Overture-derived typology, refined by floor count for residential buildings, and overridden to res_slum where the centroid falls inside a mapped slum polygon. Wards are joined by centroid, with a 100 m snap for near-boundary cases; 92,693 buildings fall outside Greater Mumbai altogether (Thane, Mira-Bhayandar, Navi Mumbai) and are dropped.

2. Population and income

Ward population (MCGM mid-2023) is distributed across residential floor area within each ward, then divided by archetype occupancy to give households. Income band is a quartile of the ward’s ready-reckoner rate, with slum-flagged buildings pinned to the lowest band — land value is a poor income proxy precisely where informal settlements sit inside expensive wards.

3. Loads and waste heat

\[ P_\text{base}(t) = \sum_\text{appliances} (\text{ownership} \times \text{rated power}) \times \sigma(t) \]

\[ UA = U A_\text{env} + 0.33\, n_\text{ACH} V \]

\[ Q_\text{cool}(t) = \big[UA \max(0, T_\text{out} - T_\text{set}) + Q_\text{sol} + Q_\text{int}\big] \times \text{occupancy} \times \text{runtime} \]

\[ Q_f(t) = P_\text{base}(t) + Q_\text{cool}(t) + \frac{Q_\text{cool}(t)}{\text{COP}} \equiv P_\text{base} + Q_\text{cool}\left(1 + \tfrac{1}{\text{COP}}\right) \]

Base electricity becomes indoor heat and is simultaneously an internal gain to the cooling load, so the two terms are physically coupled. Splitting base from cooling is what allows adoption to change anything.

Two non-building terms are added at the grid stage: human metabolism (70 W asleep, 140 W awake, following the residential occupancy schedule, placed at people’s homes) and road traffic (city-wide daily vehicle-kilometres distributed across cells by class-weighted OSM road length, shaped by a twin-peak traffic profile).

4. Indoor temperature (1R1C)

Adoption is driven by discomfort, and discomfort is felt indoors. A single-capacitance model per building gives free-running indoor air temperature:

\[ C \frac{dT_\text{in}}{dt} = UA_\text{eff}\,(T_\text{out} - T_\text{in}) + Q_\text{sol} + Q_\text{int} \]

with \(C\) = capacitance per m² × floor area, and \(UA_\text{eff}\) built from fabric plus open-window ventilation — not the closed-window ACH used for cooling load — boosted at night when outdoor air is cooler. Solar gain includes a roof term that matters only for low-rise dwellings, where roof area per dwelling is large. Integrated hourly over a repeating typical day with a five-day spin-up.

This is what makes archetypes differ. Without it, every household in the city experiences the same stimulus and the building stock does no work.

5. Feedback

\[ \Delta T(t+1) = k \big[\overline{Q_f}(t) - \overline{Q_f}(\text{baseline})\big] \]

\(\Delta T\) raises the next year’s outdoor temperature series, which raises both cooling demand and indoor discomfort. \(k\) is a transfer coefficient standing in for a surface energy balance model; it is the largest physics assumption in the project and is swept explicitly. See Limits.

## Chain

```{mermaid}
flowchart LR
  A[footprints + heights] --> B[stock: floors, area, archetype]
  T[Overture typology] --> B
  S[slum polygons] --> B
  B --> P[population + households + income band]
  W[ward population] --> P
  R[ready reckoner] --> P
  P --> L[hourly loads]
  P --> TH[1R1C indoor temperature]
  TH --> L
  L --> Q[Qf grid 100 m]
  M[metabolism] --> Q
  V[road traffic] --> Q
  TH --> ABM[household adoption ABM]
  ABM --> L
  Q -->|delta T| ABM
```

## 1. Stock

Floors are `ceil(height / storey height)` with storey height set per use class, clipped to
[1, 90]. Floor area is footprint × floors. Archetype comes from the Overture-derived
typology, refined by floor count for residential buildings, and overridden to `res_slum`
where the centroid falls inside a mapped slum polygon. Wards are joined by centroid, with
a 100 m snap for near-boundary cases; 92,693 buildings fall outside Greater Mumbai
altogether (Thane, Mira-Bhayandar, Navi Mumbai) and are dropped.

```{python}
import geopandas as gpd, pandas as pd
s = gpd.read_parquet("data/interim/stock.parquet")
t = (s.groupby("archetype")
       .agg(buildings=("building_id","size"),
            floor_area_km2=("floor_area_m2", lambda x: round(x.sum()/1e6,1)),
            median_floors=("floors","median"),
            median_height_m=("height_m", lambda x: round(x.median(),1)))
       .sort_values("buildings", ascending=False))
t
```

```{python}
w = pd.read_csv("data/processed/abm2_thermal_wards.csv")
agg = (w.assign(hh=w.households)
        .groupby("income_band")
        .apply(lambda g: pd.Series({
            "households": int(g.hh.sum()),
            "indoor mean °C": round((g.t_in_mean*g.hh).sum()/g.hh.sum(), 1),
            "indoor max °C": round((g.t_in_max*g.hh).sum()/g.hh.sum(), 1),
            "discomfort Kh": int((g.discomfort*g.hh).sum()/g.hh.sum()),
        }), include_groups=False))
agg
```
