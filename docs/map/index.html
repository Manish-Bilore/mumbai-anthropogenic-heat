What you're seeing

Each square is a 100 × 100 m cell. Its colour is the anthropogenic heat flux, Qf: the waste heat that buildings, air conditioners, people and traffic release into the outdoor air.

  • Darker = more waste heat, from pale lavender through purple to deep red, in watts per square metre of ground.
  • Year steps the adoption model: households buy ACs from 2026 to 2040.
  • Hour — drag the curve. The city peaks in the hour ending 18:00.
  • Click a cell for its value across years and hours.
  • Empty areas (SGNP, Aarey, creeks) have no buildings.
